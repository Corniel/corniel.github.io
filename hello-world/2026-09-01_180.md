# 180!
Onehunnnnnnnred and eighty!

Although I'm not much of a darts guy, the rumbling sound appeals to me. It resembles euphoria, as somebody just achieved a perfect score.

[We](https://dotnet-project-file-analyzers.github.io) did not achieve a perfect score, but with version [1.18.0](https://www.nuget.org/packages/DotNetProjectFile.Analyzers/1.18.0) we now provide 180 rules that statically analyze a wide variety of non-compiling files that are involved when building .NET projects, which is close enough.

Let's hope that [usage](https://nugettrends.com/packages?ids=DotNetProjectFile.Analyzers&months=40) within the .NET community continues to grow, and that others feel encouraged to join us. We still have a [long backlog](https://github.com/dotnet-project-file-analyzers/dotnet-project-file-analyzers/issues) with things to add and/or improve on.
